# Screens of the books

What the home page shows of Occupella itself (`TOUR` and the rent roll in `src/Landing.tsx`).

- **Where they came from:** the free PMS's own demo company (Occupella-PMS, `python -m pms.demo`),
  served from that repo's main branch on 2026-11-15 and captured in Chromium at 1280 x 800, twice
  the pixels, then scaled to 1600 x 1000 JPEGs.
- **The names:** every person, company, vendor and address is made up. The demo's placeholder
  names ("Demo Owner A", "Drew Sample") were replaced in that local copy with realistic made-up
  ones, so the pictures read as a company's books. No real tenant, owner or company appears.
- **What they claim:** only what the books do today. The owner and tenant portals are built; signing
  in to them on app.occupella.com is still Coming, and the page says so beside both.
- **Taking them again:** run the demo, sign in as its manager, owner and tenant, and capture
  `/rent-roll`, a trust bank account's Reconciliations tab, and the owner's and tenant's Overview.
