## The service

Occupella is property management software for companies that manage rentals for owners:

- **The books:** trust accounting for the money you hold for owners and tenants: rent, deposits,
  bills, owner draws, fees, bank statements and month-end reconciliation, with the reports and
  statements built from them.
- **Portals** for your owners and tenants, and pages for applicants and vendors you invite.
- **Leasing:** listings, applications, lease offers signed on Occupella's own signing page, and
  renewals.
- **Maintenance:** requests, work orders and vendors.
- **Online payments** through Stripe, where you turn them on.
- **The AI assistant,** which reads your books and the accounts you connect (such as Gmail and
  Google Calendar), answers your team's questions and drafts work for them to approve.

Occupella is a hosted service. The free, open-source PMS it is built on is a separate project
under its own license (Apache-2.0); these terms cover the hosted service only.

## Your books are yours

- You decide what goes in your books, and you can export all of them at any time: every record
  as files, and the whole ledger.
- Coming from another system, you bring your data over from the files that system exports. You
  are responsible for what those files say, and for signing off the tie-out of your opening
  balances.
- Trust records are never edited or deleted inside Occupella. A mistake is fixed with a new,
  reversing entry, and every change is kept in an audit log that names who made it.

## Money

- **Occupella holds no money and moves none.** Rent paid online is processed by Stripe, under
  Stripe's terms, into your own trust account. Owner draws and vendor payments leave through your
  bank: Occupella writes the ACH file or the checks, and you send them. Your bank pays a file each
  time it is uploaded, so upload each one once.
- **The AI never moves money.** It cannot record a payment, a charge, a bill, a draw or a fee.
  What it drafts (an email, a notice, a work order, a note) waits for a person on your team to
  approve it, and is then done as that person.
- **Your trust accounts are your responsibility.** You hold the license, the trust accounts and
  the duty to reconcile them and keep their records. Occupella gives you the tools and the checks
  (balances that must tie out, month-end approved by someone other than whoever prepared it), but
  it is not your accountant, your broker or your lawyer.

## Plans and billing

[[PLANS AND PRICES]]

Paid plans are billed in advance and renew automatically until you cancel. You can cancel at any
time from Settings, Billing, which opens our payment provider's portal. Cancelling stops the next
charge, and your account keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for. Prices
exclude any sales tax we're required to collect.

If a payment fails we keep your account running while the card is retried. If it ultimately
can't be collected, the subscription ends: your books stay intact and you can still export them.

We don't automatically refund partial months. If something goes wrong on our side, email us and a
person will sort it out. If we change a price, we'll tell your account's admins by email before it
takes effect, and you can cancel before it does.
